RUSTLINE is a round-based tactical FPS in the browser: five attackers plant the breach charge against five defending bots across a dusk container yard, with a buy economy, five weapons and grenades.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Click to play — WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, B for the buy menu, E (hold) to plant the charge inside Site A or B, G to throw a grenade, Tab for the scoreboard. First to 8 rounds wins the match.
